The Problem

The Administrative & Compliance Crisis in Social Housing

Care teams and keyworkers spend up to 40% of their shifts fighting manual spreadsheets, paper forms, and disconnected systems.

INDUSTRY PAIN POINTS
⚠️

Clipboard Chaos & Lost Records

Vital safeguarding logs written on paper clipboards go missing during shift handovers. When regulators request historical incident documentation, providers face severe compliance exposure.

🚨 73% of care providers cite documentation delays as their primary audit stress.
⏳

Delayed Safeguarding Escalation

Incidents involving emergency services, vulnerable residents, or police involvement take hours or days to format into official Clearsprings and UKVI report standards.

⏱️ Average incident reporting cycle: 3+ hours per critical event.
📉

Audit Vulnerability & Zero Signatures

Unstructured Word documents lack role-based access control (RBAC). Anyone can edit or delete text with no tamper-evident log of who authored, reviewed, or approved the report.

❌ Zero cryptographic proof of report integrity during legal inquiries.
🔄

Facility Silos & Communication Gaps

Maintenance requests, occupancy counts, keyworker rosters, and resident welfare checks reside in disconnected silos across different sites and properties.

🏢 Central management lacks a unified real-time operational picture.
Source: Supported Accommodation Quality Standards Review 2026
