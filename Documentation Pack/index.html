What is SDTracker WebCRM?

SDTracker WebCRM is the unified operations management platform for SD Commercial properties, hotels, and accommodation facilities. It replaces disjointed paper sheets and spreadsheets with a single, real-time audit, maintenance, and compliance pipeline.

The 14 Operational Modules Covered:

📊 KPI Dashboard: Real-time occupancy & welfare
📋 Daily Audits: Shift handover & H&S checklists
🛠️ Maintenance: Cat 1–4 defects & vendor dispatches
🛡️ Safeguarding: Incident reports & authority referrals
👥 Staff Directory: Shifts, contacts & DBS compliance
🏢 Commercial Welfare: Local authority metrics
📅 Compliance Tracker: Fire safety, legionella & gas
📑 Report Generator: Instant council audit exports

7. Known Issues & Quick Workarounds

Try these instant checks before raising a support ticket to continue working without delay.

1. New buttons or updated screens missing after an announced update ▼
Cause: Your browser has cached older JavaScript bundle files.
Instant Workaround: Perform a hard refresh:
  • Windows: Press Ctrl + Shift + R or Ctrl + F5
  • Mac: Press Cmd + Shift + R
  • Mobile Safari/Chrome: Close tab, clear history for the last hour, and reopen.
2. Dashboard or records appear blank or missing ▼
Cause: Top navigation site switcher is scoped to a different property or your date filter range has no data.
Instant Workaround:
  • Check the Property Selector dropdown in the top header and switch to your hotel location.
  • Check your date filter and click "Reset to This Month".
3. Mobile photo upload failed during a room audit ▼
Cause: Basement, lift, or plant room Wi-Fi dead spot temporarily interrupted upload.
Instant Workaround: Your draft is preserved automatically in local browser storage. Walk to a strong Wi-Fi area and tap "Retry Upload" or re-submit the audit.
4. Staff member cannot log in with error "User Not Active" ▼
Cause: Account status in Staff Directory is set to 'Inactive' or pending manager verification.
Instant Workaround: Ask a Site Manager or Operations Admin to check the Staff Directory, locate the staff record, and toggle status to Active.
